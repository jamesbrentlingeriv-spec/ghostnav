WHAT FLOCK MARKETING TELLS CITY COUNCILS:

"Customer data is never sold. All raw capture photos are automatically purged and deleted from servers after 30 days unless explicitly bookmarked as evidence in an active felony investigation."

THE COMPUTER SCIENCE & MACHINE LEARNING REALITY:

Before any JPEG photo is purged from secondary caches, Flock's computer vision pipeline processes each image through deep convolutional neural networks, converting your vehicle into permanent high-dimensional numerical embeddings:

  • Visual Fingerprints: Unique bumper stickers, dent profiles, roof racks, and faded paint vectors.
  • Routine Pattern-of-Life: Time-of-day travel frequencies, recurring route intersections, and companion vehicle clustering.
  • Permanent Model Weights: Neural network classification models remain trained on your driving habits forever.
Key Takeaway: Deleting the raw JPEG does not delete the mathematical representation of your movement. Your travel behavior is permanently encoded into proprietary predictive models without your informed consent.
